Privacy Policy - Idea2LLD

Last updated: March 2025

Welcome to Idea2LLD, an AI-powered platform that transforms startup ideas into structured low-level system designs. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal and project-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • What information we collect.
  • How your data is used and protected.
  • Your rights and choices.
  • How to contact us.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register or use Idea2LLD, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number (for authentication and communication)
  • User role (Founder / Investor)
  • Profile information you choose to provide

1.2 Project & Idea Data

  • Startup ideas and descriptions
  • Generated low-level designs (LLD)
  • Architecture details, diagrams, and system flows
  • Chat-based design modifications and version history

1.3 Usage & Technical Data

  • Device information and IP address
  • App usage statistics
  • Interaction timestamps
  • Error logs and performance diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Authenticate users securely
  • Generate AI-powered low-level designs
  • Improve accuracy and relevance of AI responses
  • Enable collaboration between founders and investors
  • Maintain project history and version control
  • Enhance app stability, security, and performance

3. Data Security

We take data security seriously and apply:

  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Encrypted network communication (HTTPS)
  • Controlled access to user projects
  • Regular monitoring and security updates

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your profile
  • View or delete your projects
  • Request correction or removal of personal data
  • Delete your account permanently

5. Third-Party Services

  • Cloud services for authentication and storage
  • Analytics tools to improve app experience
  • AI services for design generation

All third-party services are required to comply with strict data protection standards.

6. Children's Privacy

Idea2LLD is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If such data is identified, it will be deleted immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app and on our official platforms.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us:

  • Email: support@idea2lld.com
  • App: Use the “Contact Us” option in the app settings.
